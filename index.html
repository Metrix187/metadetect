    What this can (and can’t) do

    How it works

    Smart glasses and recording wearables chatter over Bluetooth Low Energy. Each advert carries a manufacturer company id baked in by the Bluetooth SIG — and unlike a MAC address, that id isn’t randomized. We watch for the ids and names that belong to known recorders.

    What it detects

    • Camera glasses — Ray-Ban Meta, Snap Spectacles, cheap AI cam-glasses.
    • Audio glasses — Echo Frames, XREAL, Even Realities and friends.
    • AI recorders — pendants/pins like Limitless, Plaud, Omi (name-based, less reliable).

    The honest caveats

    • Lots of gear only broadcasts loudly when it powers on, pairs, or leaves its case — not continuously while filming. A silent “all clear” is not a guarantee.
    • Can’t see purely wired or offline cameras that keep their radios dark.
    • On iPhone/iPad there’s no Web Bluetooth at all — Apple blocks it. Use Android Chrome or the ESP32 build.
    • Even on Android you must switch on chrome://flags → “Experimental Web Platform features.”
    • Signatures drift. New gadgets appear. Treat this as a smoke alarm, not a force field.

    Pause

    Pause fully stops scanning and logging — nothing gets watched or recorded on your end until you resume. Handy when you’re somewhere you trust and don’t want the buzzing.
